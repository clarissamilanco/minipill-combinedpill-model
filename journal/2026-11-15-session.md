## What I learned

- **Structure**: `params.py` holds the drugs and their sources, `pk.py` holds the model, `scripts/fit_*.py` fit parameters to the labels, `tests/` checks the math and the label match, `export_comparison_data.py` writes the results into the HTML page. Libraries: NumPy, SciPy (`solve_ivp` to solve the equations, `least_squares` / `brentq` to fit), pytest.
- **Gamma**: clearance is scaled by (57 / SHBG)^γ. γ = 0 means SHBG has no effect; the fitted γ = 1.33 means a 1% rise in SHBG cuts clearance by about 1.33%, so at the SHBG plateau clearance is about 52% of normal (the label's CL/F falls to 53%). It's above 1 because SHBG stands for the whole estrogen effect, not only binding.
- **`clearance_L_per_h` isn't used in the app**: the HTML pages only show numbers precomputed by `simulate`, which uses `k10` directly. Clearance is used in the levonorgestrel fit (AUC = F·dose / CL), printed by the fit scripts, and checked in a test.
- **`simulate`**: splits time at every pill and every estrogen-off time, adds each pill to the gut, lets `solve_ivp` (LSODA) solve the smooth stretch in between, and carries the end state `y` into the next stretch. It tracks amounts (mg); concentration = blood amount / V_L, computed once at the end.
- **`args`** = (ka, F, k10, k12, k21) for the one drug simulated. F is a fraction, not a rate. V_L is left out because the equations work in amounts. Order must match `_rhs`.
- **`bateman`**: exact one-compartment single-dose curve, C(t) = F·D·ka / (V(ka − k)) · (e^(−kt) − e^(−ka·t)). e^(−kt) is the slow elimination tail; e^(−ka·t) fades as absorption finishes. Setting the slope to 0 gives `tmax`.
- **`exact_single_dose`**: the same for two compartments, by writing the model as y' = M y and using the eigenvalues of M (−ka, −α, −β). Blood amount is a sum of three exponentials.
- **Which function where**: `bateman` only in tests; `exact_single_dose` in tests and in the norethindrone and levonorgestrel fits (fast, and the labels describe a single pill); `simulate` makes the app curves and is used in the SHBG fit (many pills plus SHBG). The fits compare model predictions with real label measurements.

## Demo run (three norethindrone pills at 0, 24, 48 h)

- Just before and at the 24 h pill, blood (0.0024 mg) and tissue (0.0117 mg) are identical; only the gut jumps by 0.35 mg. The new pill lands on top of what's left.
- Build-up: peak 4723 → 4839 pg/mL; low before the next pill 129 → 144 → 146 pg/mL, levelling off fast (short half-life).
- At the low points the tissue holds about 5× the blood amount, leaking back slowly.
- Every row: sum of compartments = amount swallowed; first pass = 35% of each pill (F = 0.65).
