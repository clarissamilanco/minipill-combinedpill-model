**How SHBG works in the model**
- **SHBG rises while active pills are taken and drifts back down in the placebo week.** Its curve goes exactly through Alesse's measured values: 57, 81 and 93 nmol/L on days 1, 6 and 21. It gets halfway to its plateau in about 3 days.
- **More SHBG means slower clearance.** Levonorgestrel's clearance scales as (57 ÷ SHBG)^1.33. I fitted the 1.33 to the measured exposure (AUC) on days 6 and 21, and the model matches it within 4%.
