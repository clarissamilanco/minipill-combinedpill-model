## Findings

- **One compartment fails the 24 h check, as predicted**: 7.6-fold fall from peak to 24 h vs. the label's 37-fold. With the label's 4 L/kg volume it also underpredicts the peak ~5-fold (851 vs. 4,817 pg/mL).
- **Two compartments fix it.** Fitted (F = 0.65 fixed from label): ka 0.911 /h, V1 18.7 L, k10 0.520, k12 0.320, k21 0.155 /h. Every label value within 3%: Tmax 1.18 h, Cmax 4,724, Cavg 914, C24 129 pg/mL, half-life 7.8 h. The steep early drop is drug moving into tissues (half-life ~0.8 h), not leaving the body.
- **Fit caveat**: from 30 starting points the fit always lands at ka ≈ alpha (~0.91 /h). The label's summary values can't separate absorption from the fast tissue phase. The curve, 24 h level and terminal phase are well determined.
- **Label clearance/volume don't match its own concentrations**: fit implies ~230 L/day and ~1 L/kg vs. label's ~600 L/day and ~4 L/kg (probably different studies). An AUC check gives ~240 L/day too.
- **Barely any build-up**: steady-state trough 146 pg/mL, 1.13× the first-dose trough.
- **Late pill (two-compartment model)**: usual daily low 146 pg/mL. 3 h late → min 112 (23% below) for 3 extra hours; 12 h late → 50 (66% below) for 12 h; skipped → 17 (88% below) for 25 h. Extra time below the usual low equals the delay, because each pill only covers ~24 h.

## Problems found in my sources / proposal

- `cdc_missedpill_guidance.pdf` is the **progestin-only** sheet; its "<48 h / ≥48 h" columns are for **drospirenone** POPs. I need the CDC combined-pill sheet to cite the 48 h rule.
- README's levonorgestrel accumulation ratio was 2.4; with a 30 h half-life it's **2.35** (36 h → ~2.7). Fixed.
- "3- to 4-fold" levonorgestrel rise is from **Levette**; Alesse (0.10 mg, my validation pill) shows ~2-fold (Cmax 2.75 → 6.0, AUC 35 → 68). Use Alesse numbers for the Alesse check.
- Parameter sheet is missing norethindrone **F ≈ 65%** (now in `params.py`).
- **Alesse Table I has SHBG and CL/F on days 1, 6, 21** (SHBG 57 → 81 → 93 nmol/L; CL/F 53.7 → 40.8 → 28.4 mL/h/kg). Use these to calibrate the SHBG effect instead of tuning by eye.
- Levette gives levonorgestrel half-life ~24 h vs. 30 h (Seasonale) and 36 h (Alesse).
- Parameter sheet cites a "Heather label" for volume and clearance; the norethindrone label in `sources/` has the same numbers.
