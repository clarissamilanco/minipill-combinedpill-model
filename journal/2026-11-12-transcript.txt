  What the sheet says, for your late-pill comparison
  - Less than 48 hours since the pill was due: take it as soon as possible; no
    backup contraception is needed. CDC calls it "late" under 24 hours and
    "missed" from 24 to under 48 hours, but the advice is the same.
  - 48 hours or more (two or more pills missed): use condoms or abstain until
    pills have been taken for 7 days in a row.

  So the README's "up to 48 hours late without backup" matches the source. In
  the model, your 48 h scenario means two consecutive missed pills.

  The sheet was published in August 2024. I haven't checked whether CDC has
  changed or taken down that guidance since, so it's worth a look at
