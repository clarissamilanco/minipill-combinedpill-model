## Findings

- **CDC combined-pill sheet (CS39620-C, 2024)**: under 48 h since a pill was due, no backup needed (called "late" under 24 h, "missed" 24–48 h). At 48 h or more (two or more pills missed), condoms or abstinence until 7 days of pills in a row. So the README's "up to 48 hours late" is right, and the 48 h scenario means two missed pills.
- **One compartment fails for levonorgestrel too**: with the label's own CL/F and Vz/F, the day-1 peak is 0.69 ng/mL vs. 2.75 measured (4-fold too low).
- **Two compartments match all four day-1 values within 2%**: Tmax 1.6 h, Cmax 2.75 ng/mL, AUC 35.2 ng·h/mL, half-life 34.3 h. Fitted: V1 16.7 L, k10 0.170, k12 0.339, k21 0.066 /h (ka 0.8 assumed, F = 1).
- **The ka assumption doesn't change the answers**: any ka from 0.4 to 1.2 /h fits exactly and gives a 24 h level of 0.30–0.33 ng/mL and the same 38% late-pill drop (`fit_levonorgestrel.py --profile`).
- **The gap SHBG has to explain**: without SHBG the model's day-21 AUC is 35 ng·h/mL vs. 68 measured, and the low before the next pill is 0.84 vs. 1.9 ng/mL. The AUC gap (×1.9) matches the label's CL/F fall from day 1 to day 21 (53.7 → 28.4 mL/h/kg, ×1.9).
- **Late pill, levonorgestrel without SHBG (preliminary)**: 3 h late → 6% below the usual low; 24 h late → 38%; two pills missed (48 h) → 62%. Compare norethindrone: 3 h late → 23%, skipped → 88%.
- The label's day-1 AUC and CL/F imply a body weight of ~53 kg, not my assumed 60 kg. Doesn't matter for levonorgestrel since everything is in L/h directly.

## Decisions

- **One comparison page** (`app/pill_comparison.html`) with both drugs on shared axes: ng/mL for the curves (norethindrone pg/mL ÷ 1000), and **% of each pill's own usual low** for the late-pill chart so the two can be read on one scale. Single-drug pages kept.
- **SHBG model**: SHBG is a sixth state that moves toward a plateau while estrogen is "on" (24 h after each active pill) and back to baseline otherwise; levonorgestrel clearance scales as (57 / SHBG)^γ. Ethinyl estradiol itself isn't simulated.
- **Fit to concentrations, not the label's CL/F.** The label's day-6 CL/F assumes steady state, which day 6 isn't, so I fitted γ to the measured day-6 and day-21 AUCs by simulation.
- **Page data generated from Python.** With SHBG there's no closed-form curve, so `scripts/export_comparison_data.py` simulates the combined-pill scenarios and writes them into the page (no more hand-copied numbers for this page).
- **Next steps prioritized for Nov 20**: check the page in a browser, host it as the app (GitHub Pages), tighten the README, video; then a sensitivity check.

## Findings

- **SHBG and clearance don't move in step**: SHBG levels off fast (half-way in 3.2 days; 57 → 93.5 nmol/L plateau) while the label's CL/F keeps falling to day 21 (half-way ~5.5 days). A single SHBG–clearance power law needs γ = 0.78 for day 6 but 1.30 for day 21 using the label's CL/F. Fitting to the AUCs directly gives one γ = 1.33 that matches both (48.0 vs. 46.0, 66.8 vs. 68.3 ng·h/mL).
- **Unfitted check passes**: week-3 low before the next pill 2.04 ng/mL vs. 1.9 measured (+7%). Usual low at day 15 is 1.91 ng/mL.
- **Week-3 peak is low**: 4.94 vs. 6.0 ± 2.65 ng/mL. Likely because the label's Vz/F shrinks (2.66 → 1.43 L/kg) and the model keeps volume fixed.
- **Not only binding**: the label's unbound levonorgestrel also rose (+83%) and unbound clearance fell (2.79 → 1.57 L/h/kg); free fraction barely changed (1.92 → 1.78%). So in the model SHBG stands for the whole estrogen effect on clearance.
- **Main result (day-15 pill, dip below each pill's usual low)**:

  | Day-15 pill | Minipill | Combined pill (with SHBG) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 3 h late | 23% | 4% |
  | 12 h late | 66% | 15% |
  | 1 pill missed | 88% | 28% |
  | 2 pills missed (48 h) | 99% | 49% |

  **At each pill's CDC limit the dips are similar**: minipill 3 h late 23%; combined pill one pill missed (under 48 h) 28%. Supports the hypothesis, with the caveat that hormone level stands in for protection (the minipill also works via cervical mucus).
