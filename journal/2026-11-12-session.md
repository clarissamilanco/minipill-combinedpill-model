## Findings

- **CDC combined-pill sheet (CS39620-C, 2024)**: under 48 h since a pill was due, no backup needed (called "late" under 24 h, "missed" 24–48 h). At 48 h or more (two or more pills missed), condoms or abstinence until 7 days of pills in a row. So the README's "up to 48 hours late" is right, and the 48 h scenario means two missed pills.
- **One compartment fails for levonorgestrel too**: with the label's own CL/F and Vz/F, the day-1 peak is 0.69 ng/mL vs. 2.75 measured (4-fold too low).
- **Two compartments match all four day-1 values within 2%**: Tmax 1.6 h, Cmax 2.75 ng/mL, AUC 35.2 ng·h/mL, half-life 34.3 h. Fitted: V1 16.7 L, k10 0.170, k12 0.339, k21 0.066 /h (ka 0.8 assumed, F = 1).
- **The ka assumption doesn't change the answers**: any ka from 0.4 to 1.2 /h fits exactly and gives a 24 h level of 0.30–0.33 ng/mL and the same 38% late-pill drop (`fit_levonorgestrel.py --profile`).
- **The gap SHBG has to explain**: without SHBG the model's day-21 AUC is 35 ng·h/mL vs. 68 measured, and the low before the next pill is 0.84 vs. 1.9 ng/mL. The AUC gap (×1.9) matches the label's CL/F fall from day 1 to day 21 (53.7 → 28.4 mL/h/kg, ×1.9).
- **Late pill, levonorgestrel without SHBG (preliminary)**: 3 h late → 6% below the usual low; 24 h late → 38%; two pills missed (48 h) → 62%. Compare norethindrone: 3 h late → 23%, skipped → 88%.
- The label's day-1 AUC and CL/F imply a body weight of ~53 kg, not my assumed 60 kg. Doesn't matter for levonorgestrel since everything is in L/h directly.
