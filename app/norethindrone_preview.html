Minipill model · norethindrone 0.35 mg

Norethindrone levels in the blood

The minipill's hormone peaks about an hour after each pill and is mostly gone a day later. These charts compare the model with the drug label, then show what a late pill does.

One pill: model vs. label

The one-compartment model uses the label's 7.7 h half-life but falls only 7.6-fold in 24 hours. The label shows a 37-fold fall. Adding a tissue compartment, fitted to the label, closes the gap.

When a pill is late

Daily pills at the same time each day, then one pill taken late. The dotted line is the usual daily low, the level just before an on-time pill. The question is how far, and for how long, a late pill pushes levels below it. Uses the two-compartment model.
